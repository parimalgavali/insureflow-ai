# US-08: Explain unclear coverage

Customers see whether coverage needs missing facts, relevant policy wording, resolution of conflicting information, or human review. Each explanation includes a next step in the selected interface language. No confidence percentage is invented.

The composer reports a structured uncertainty reason and explains it in the conversation language. Any reported uncertainty prevents a definite coverage badge. Unsupported clause references force human review; an answer without any supported citation is replaced by the no-evidence response. The UI always labels model-term guidance as preliminary and explains that a citation does not establish coverage or a payment decision.

Older unclear cards use a general human-review explanation; missing facts and stale assessments take precedence. Conflicts are reported from the assessment, not guessed from keywords. Citation validation checks source identifiers, not semantic entailment; actual contract interpretation remains a human responsibility.
